\noindent\textbf{Research Highlights}

\medskip
\noindent\textit{These Four First: A Robust Endogenous Baseline for Relational Event Sequences}

\bigskip
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.5em]

  \item Four endogenous terms --- IOviolation, SndInitiation, Repetition, and
        RecRecencyRank --- appear in the greedy top-four selection path of 55--94\%
        of domains across 217 interaction sequences spanning ten social settings.

  \item The four-term baseline recovers approximately 97\% of the AIC improvement
        achievable by the full twelve-term candidate set, with a 22-percentage-point
        frequency gap separating the fourth-ranked term from the fifth.

  \item Imitation and triadic closure terms, despite prominent roles in the network
        literature, do not belong in the baseline: their signal is absorbed by
        forward-dyad history once SndInitiation and Repetition enter the model.

  \item The contribution of imitation is ecologically contingent: it is a serious
        baseline candidate in high-visibility settings (40\% top-four frequency)
        but negligible in private communication contexts (5\%).

  \item The consistent early selection of SndInitiation --- a binary absorbing state
        partitioning events into first-contact and return-contact regimes --- motivates
        separable relational event models with distinct parameter vectors for each regime.

\end{itemize}
